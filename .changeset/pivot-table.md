Add `PivotTable` to `data`: a cross-tab of flat records with sum, count, distinct count, mean, median, min, max or custom aggregates, date bucketing, filters, collapsible row groups, subtotals and totals, rendered as a `<table>` whose cells name every header through `headers`, with a menu-driven field configurator. The `pivot` engine is exported too.

`MenuSeparator` and `MenuRadioGroup` in a `DropdownMenu` or `ContextMenu` now lay out as one rule and one column of choices, rather than taking daisyUI's menu-item grid.
