Add `AngleSlider`: a round dial for an angle or bearing. An APG slider that wraps from 359° to 0°, dragged anywhere on the dial or stepped with the arrow, Page and Home/End keys; marks, `restrictToMarks`, compass marks with the compass point in `aria-valuetext`, a part range, `zero` north or east, `onCommit` once per drag, and the value posted under `name`. Also exports `compass8`, `compass16` and the `AngleZero` type.
