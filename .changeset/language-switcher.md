`LanguageSwitcher`: a locale picker that names each language in its own language through `Intl.DisplayNames`. It is one toggle naming the other language when there are two locales and a `DropdownMenu` when there are more, with links (`href`) or an in-place `onSelect`. `languageEndonyms` exposes the naming on its own.
