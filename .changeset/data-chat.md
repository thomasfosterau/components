Add `ChatBubble` and `MessageThread` (data family). A thread is a region named by `label` around an ordered list; `live` makes it a polite log. Each message is an article named by its author and time ("Ada Lovelace, 3 minutes ago"), with a `<time datetime>`, a decorative avatar, a status footer and a `tone` from the new `chatBubbleTone` table.
