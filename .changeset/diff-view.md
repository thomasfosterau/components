Add `DiffView`, a line-by-line diff of two texts in a unified or side-by-side table, with word highlights in changed lines, collapsed unchanged runs and line numbers. Every changed line is prefixed "Added:" or "Removed:" for screen readers. The line diff it runs on (`diffLines`, `diffBlocks`, `splitRows`, `diffWords`, `diffSequences`) is exported.
